I've spent the last few years building case studies in Notion — and it's been a great way to organise my work. But when you're job hunting, you need something that feels intentional: fast to load, easy to navigate, and built for the people reviewing your application.

## What I'm optimising for

**Clarity over complexity.** Recruiters and hiring managers often have minutes, not hours. Each case study leads with the problem, my role, and the outcome — so the value is visible before they scroll.

**Research credibility.** My strongest work sits at the intersection of user research and UI design. Co-design workshops, usability testing, and mixed-method research aren't footnotes — they're the story.

**Sharing on LinkedIn.** This blog is where I'll publish shorter insights from my projects: what I learned, what I'd do differently, and patterns I think other designers might find useful.
